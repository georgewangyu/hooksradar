## Featured Hooks

- [Blunt Reality Confession](https://hooksradar.snackoverflowgeorge.com/hooks/blunt-reality-confession)
  - Formula: Let's come clean about the reality of [thing].
  - Strength: source-observed
  - Proof type: direct-claim
  - Use cases: ai-workflow, career, on-call, creator-process, money
  - Why it matters: Signals a correction and makes the viewer expect a less polished truth.

- [Emerging Realization](https://hooksradar.snackoverflowgeorge.com/hooks/emerging-realization)
  - Formula: People are beginning to realize that [weird / pattern-interrupt statement].
  - Strength: source-observed
  - Proof type: pattern-interrupt
  - Use cases: trend, ai-workflow, creator-strategy, career, opinion
  - Why it matters: Makes a strange thesis feel obvious by the end.

- [Hidden Cost Reversal](https://hooksradar.snackoverflowgeorge.com/hooks/hidden-cost-reversal)
  - Formula: Everyone tells you [obvious advice], but no one tells you [hidden cost / lonely truth].
  - Strength: source-observed
  - Proof type: contrarian
  - Use cases: career, ai-workflow, creator-process, money, life-tradeoff
  - Why it matters: Starts with accepted advice and flips to the overlooked cost.

- [On-Call Interruption](https://hooksradar.snackoverflowgeorge.com/hooks/on-call-interruption)
  - Formula: Getting paged as an on-call software engineer [during/while/at X].
  - Strength: owned-proven
  - Proof type: pattern-interrupt
  - Use cases: career, on-call, day-in-the-life, story, software-engineering
  - Why it matters: Normal life collides with production responsibility.

- [Timed Outcome Challenge](https://hooksradar.snackoverflowgeorge.com/hooks/timed-outcome-challenge)
  - Formula: Is it possible to [outcome] in [extremely short time frame]?
  - Strength: source-observed
  - Proof type: question
  - Use cases: build-in-public, ai-workflow, creator-process, learning, experiment
  - Why it matters: Turns a process video into a visible wager.

- [AI Demo Meets Production Reality](https://hooksradar.snackoverflowgeorge.com/hooks/ai-demo-production-reality)
  - Formula: The AI did [impressive demo]. Then I asked it to handle [boring production reality].
  - Strength: owned-adjacent
  - Proof type: contrarian
  - Use cases: ai-workflow, software-engineering, product, debugging, code-review
  - Why it matters: Moves from magic demo to senior-engineer reality.

- [Credibility Stack](https://hooksradar.snackoverflowgeorge.com/hooks/credibility-stack)
  - Formula: My credibility? [credential 1], [credential 2], [credential 3].
  - Strength: source-observed
  - Proof type: social-proof
  - Use cases: education, career, ai-workflow, creator-process
  - Why it matters: Compresses trust before a teaching or opinion video.

- [Explain It Simply](https://hooksradar.snackoverflowgeorge.com/hooks/explain-it-simply)
  - Formula: [Thing] explained to a 5-year-old.
  - Strength: source-observed
  - Proof type: education
  - Use cases: education, ai-workflow, career, money, technical-explainer
  - Why it matters: Promises clarity on a dense topic without requiring expertise.
